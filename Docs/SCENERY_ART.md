# Wuxia Environment Art

Sixty dedicated story paintings now accompany the original seven battle themes, three menu paintings and eleven journey maps. All are landscape images suited to the game’s horizontal screen. Generated originals remain outside the project.

## Story backgrounds (stages 7–66)

Each stage from 7 through 66 has its own full-screen landscape painting at `Resources/Art/Backgrounds/stage_07.png` through `stage_66.png`. `SceneryArt.StageKey` selects it consistently for battles, story panels, region previews and the home journey card, across all difficulties. Quest rehearsals now use their referenced story stage's painting; the tower keeps `battle_academy`.

The new paintings use a consistent semi-realistic wuxia style with restrained ink-wash depth, varied natural light, timber architecture and stone or earth foregrounds. Characters, UI, writing and logos are omitted. Landmarks and props stay around the edges and in the rear, leaving the lower central area available for combatants. Backgrounds interpret the source setting and mood; decorative architecture, lighting and vegetation do not establish additional canon.

Stages 1–6 retain the original `battle_forest`, `battle_manor` and `battle_road` paintings. The original seven battle themes remain available to menus and the tower. `stage_01.png` is intentionally absent. Invalid stage numbers still resolve to `battle_road`.

All new PNGs retain their original dimensions. Import metadata disables mipmaps and CPU readability, clamps wrap modes, preserves NPOT dimensions, and enables platform texture compression with a 2048 maximum texture size.

## Journey maps in the stage list

`SceneryArt.JourneyPageCount` derives the map count from the current story-stage count using the shared `StagesPerJourney` constant (six stops). `StageMapPanel` uses those same values. All eleven pages now have distinct `world_journey_01.png`–`world_journey_11.png` resources. The previous fixed six-page clamp caused every page from stages 31–36 onward to reuse the sixth painting; the upper bound now follows the actual page count.

The first five maps (stages 1–30) are retained. Map 06 is repainted and maps 07–11 are new. These are high oblique landscape panoramas for the route UI, separate from the per-stage battle paintings in the inspector. Landmarks are decorative; paths, stage nodes, labels and progression indicators are drawn by the UI rather than baked into the art. The route does not establish literal geography.

Dark overlays and existing card surfaces separate text from the art. `BackgroundCover` crops centered UVs to fill each rectangle with the original aspect ratio, without changing the image quad, blocking touches, introducing letterboxes or stretching the scenery. It refreshes when the viewport resizes, including safe-area changes.

## Validation

The asset audit checks all 60 stage PNGs for successful decoding, landscape aspect, opaque pixels, distinct pixel hashes, unique Unity GUIDs, matching names and mobile-friendly import settings. A build against Unity reference assemblies type-checks the actual `SceneryArt.cs`. A separate lookup audit uses minimal Unity type stand-ins and a captured resource provider to exercise that same source with actual Core stage/quest data across all stages, difficulties, quests, tower and invalid indices. Neither audit runs Unity rendering or the Unity resource importer. `Tests/UI/SceneryArtTests.cs` checks actual resource loading, distinct backgrounds, difficulty consistency, quest mapping and cover geometry in the Unity editor.

The cloud cannot execute Unity rendering or Android touch input. Verify crop composition and character/text contrast in Editor/Android, especially 4:3 and 20:9 screens and the darkest cave scenes.
